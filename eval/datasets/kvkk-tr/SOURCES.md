# kvkk-tr — Sources

Turkish half of the article-aligned KVKK parallel corpus. Every file in
`corpus/` has a same-named counterpart in `../kvkk-en/corpus/` containing the
same legal provision (one article per file). Generated by
`scripts/fetch_kvkk.py`; do not hand-edit `corpus/`.

Retrieved: 2026-09-26

## Licence / status

Statutes and regulations published in the Official Gazette are not protected
works under Law No. 5846 on Intellectual and Artistic Works (FSEK) art. 31 and
may be freely reproduced. The texts below are the official Turkish texts.

## Processing notes

- Articles are split on `MADDE n` / `GEÇİCİ MADDE n`; the article title is the
  heading line that precedes it. Chapter (BÖLÜM) headings are dropped.
- mevzuat.gov.tr amendment footnotes (e.g. KHK 703 wording changes) and their
  in-text markers are removed. Inline amendment notes such as
  `(Değişik:2/3/2024-7499/34 md.)` are part of the consolidated text and kept.
- Law 6698: the staff-position table annex ((I) sayılı cetvel) is skipped.
- Law 6698 geçici madde 2 and 3 have no title in the Turkish text.
