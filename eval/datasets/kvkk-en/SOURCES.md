# kvkk-en — Sources

English half of the article-aligned KVKK parallel corpus. Every file in
`corpus/` has a same-named counterpart in `../kvkk-tr/corpus/` containing the
same legal provision (one article per file). Generated by
`scripts/fetch_kvkk.py`; do not hand-edit `corpus/`.

Retrieved: 2026-09-26

## Licence / status

These are English translations published by the Personal Data Protection
Authority (KVKK) on kvkk.gov.tr. They are **not official/binding texts**: the
law and the two 2017 by-laws carry the note "This is an English translation.
In case of any difference in meaning between the original Turkish text and the
English translation, the Turkish text shall apply"; the 2024 transfer by-law is
labelled "a courtesy translation without binding nature ... the original
Turkish text shall prevail". The underlying legislation is freely usable
(FSEK art. 31); the translations are reproduced here for research use with
attribution to the KVKK Authority.

## Known source quirks (kept as published, not "fixed")

- Law 6698 art. 2: the translation numbers its only paragraph "(2)" (Turkish: "(1)").
- Law 6698 art. 7 and art. 30: first paragraph has no "(1)" marker in the translation.
- Law 6698 art. 18: first paragraph is marked "1)"; `sicil` art. 9 para 2 and
  art. 11 paras 4–5 are marked "2)", "4)", "5)".
- `silme` final article is printed as "ARTICLE 8" on the site; it is the
  enforcement article 15 (Turkish "MADDE 15 – Yürütme") and is filed as
  `silme-md-15.md`.
- On the site, provisional article 3 of Law 6698 appears between arts. 18 and 19;
  it is filed as `6698-gecici-03.md`.

## Processing notes

- Articles are split on `ARTICLE n` / `PROVISIONAL ARTICLE n`; the article title
  is the heading line preceding it. Chapter/Part headings are dropped.
- HTML ordered lists (definitions in Law 6698 art. 3, provisional art. 3) lose
  their letters/numbers in plain-text extraction; labels are reconstructed from
  the `<ol start/type>` attributes.
- Sentences split across two `<p>` elements in the source are re-joined, and
  numbered paragraphs run together in one `<p>` are split.
- The transfer by-law PDF is converted with poppler `pdftotext`; lines are
  re-joined into paragraphs, page numbers dropped, and "ByLaw" (de-hyphenated
  line break) restored to "By-Law".
- Law 6698: the staff-position table annex (Table No. (I)) is skipped.
